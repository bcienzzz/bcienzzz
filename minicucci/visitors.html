Local tips

How to enjoy it like a local

  • Breakfast

    Italians have breakfast standing at the counter: a cappuccino and a cornetto. Try the lemon one.

  • The rum baba

    The babà is Naples' most famous pastry, soaked in a sweet syrup. A must-try.

  • Lemon flavours

    Lemons are the taste of the Gulf of Naples — perfect on a hot beach day.

  • Gelato

    End your evening stroll the Italian way: with a cone or a cup.
